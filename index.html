Chitosan is a natural biopolymer derived from marine shellfish shells — antimicrobial, film-forming, and deeply biocompatible.

Texture:Silky, Absorbent
Source:Marine Shellfish
Benefits:Healing, Hydrating
The Journey

From Ocean to Skin

01

The Marine World

Along India's pristine coasts, shellfish thrive in mineral-rich waters. Their shells — often discarded as waste — contain chitin, the second most abundant natural polysaccharide on Earth.

🌊
02

The Extraction

Through precise deacetylation, chitin transforms into chitosan — a remarkable cationic polymer. Our biotech process ensures pharmaceutical-grade purity while respecting the environment.

🔬
03

The Transformation

Chitosan's positive charge bonds with skin's negatively charged surface, forming a protective biofilm. It delivers actives deeper, locks moisture, and provides natural antimicrobial defense.

✨
Skin Science

Clinically Proven Benefits

💧
Deep Hydration

Forms a moisture-locking biofilm that reduces water loss by up to 40%, keeping skin plump.

🛡️
Antimicrobial

Natural cationic properties inhibit bacterial growth — ideal for acne-prone and sensitive skin.

🎬
Film-Forming

Creates a smooth biopolymer matrix that improves skin texture and enhances ingredient delivery.

⚗️
Wound Healing

Accelerates skin regeneration and collagen synthesis for faster healing and reduced scarring.

✨
Hair Conditioning

Repairs cuticle damage, reduces frizz, and adds luminous shine to all hair types.

🌊
Biocompatible

100% natural origin, biodegradable, and safe for all skin types including the most sensitive.

Mango Butter is a rich emollient cold-pressed from the kernels of Indian Alphonso mangoes — velvety, vitamin-rich, and deeply nourishing for skin and hair.

Texture:Velvety
Source:Indian Alphonso
Benefits:Deep Hydration
The Journey

From Grove to Glow

01

The Mango Grove

In the sun-drenched orchards of Ratnagiri and Devgad, the legendary Alphonso mango ripens to perfection. The fertile Konkan coast of Maharashtra produces some of the world's finest mangoes.

🌳
02

The Seed Harvest

After the fruit is savored, the seeds — usually discarded — are carefully collected. Our seed-to-butter program creates economic value for farming communities while eliminating agricultural waste.

🥭
03

The Golden Butter

Cold-pressed using traditional methods with modern refinement, the mango kernel is transformed into a luxuriously smooth butter rich in oleic acid, stearic acid, and vitamins A, C & E.

✨
Skin Science

Golden Benefits

🧴
Deep Moisture

Penetrates deeply without greasiness — provides long-lasting hydration for the driest skin.

☀️
Vitamin Rich

Naturally abundant in vitamins A, C & E — powerful antioxidants that brighten and protect.

🌟
Anti-Aging

High oleic acid content supports skin elasticity and reduces the appearance of fine lines.

💆
Hair Nourishment

Deeply conditions hair, reduces breakage, and adds extraordinary softness and shine.

🌺
Eczema Relief

Soothing fatty acid profile provides natural relief for eczema, psoriasis, and dermatitis.

💋
Lip Care

Perfect for luxury lip balms and treatments — heals and moisturizes chapped lips beautifully.

Kokum Butter is Ayurveda's most treasured skin butter — extracted from Garcinia indica seeds found in the mystical forests of India's Western Ghats.

Texture:Rich, Dense
Source:Western Ghats, India
Benefits:Deep Repair
The Journey

From Ancient Forest to Skin

01

The Sacred Tree

Deep within the biodiversity-rich Western Ghats — a UNESCO World Heritage site — the Garcinia indica tree bears the kokum fruit. Revered in Ayurveda for millennia, it thrives in the misty forests of Goa, Karnataka, and Maharashtra.

🌲
02

The Tribal Harvest

Tribal communities with generations of forest knowledge carefully harvest kokum during the April–May season. Seeds are extracted and sun-dried in the traditional way — preserving all natural phytonutrients.

🫐
03

The Pure Butter

Cold-pressed at low temperatures, kokum butter is one of nature's hardest butters — stearic-acid rich, oxidatively stable, and uniquely effective for anhydrous formulations and deep skin repair.

✨
Skin Science

Ancient Wisdom, Modern Results

🔮
Deep Repair

Highest stearic acid of any natural butter — deeply regenerates cracked heels, elbows, and very dry skin.

❄️
Skin-Temp Melt

Melts exactly at body temperature for a silky, non-greasy application that absorbs fully into skin.

🌸
Anti-Inflammatory

Garcinol content provides exceptional relief for rosacea, psoriasis, and chronically irritated skin.

⚡
Cell Regeneration

Promotes cellular turnover and collagen synthesis for firmer, more youthful-looking skin.

🧊
High Stability

Exceptional oxidative stability — ideal for formulations requiring long shelf life without preservatives.

💜
Lip & Cuticle Care

Transforms rough, damaged lips and cuticles with a single application — Ayurveda's finest.
